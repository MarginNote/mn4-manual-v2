# Document Review: Occlusion, Cloze, and Recall Mode

## 1 What Is `Recall Mode`?

> **Recall mode: an immersive review experience for building long-term memory**
>
> Create occlusions and clozes in **cards, rectangular areas, or text passages** to practice retrieving knowledge and strengthen memory connections. You can **choose the area and style of occlusion**, from a single formula to an entire passage, to focus your recall practice on exactly what you want to learn.
>
> Turning on recall mode **activates occlusions throughout the study set**. Tap to reveal answers, repeating the cycle of recalling, checking, and reinforcing what you know.
>
> ![Occluded content with recall mode enabled](image/a383fb371f97a391.webp)

## 2 Turn On Recall Mode

### 2.1 Turn On Recall Mode in Immersive Mode

Enter `Immersive mode`, then tap the person icon on the `bottom toolbar` to enable `Recall mode`. See the recall mode toolbar for its available controls.

![Turning on recall mode in immersive mode](image/a365a2c5de46ee19.webp)

### 2.2 Turn On Legacy Recall Mode

- In MarginNote, go to `Settings` > `LEGACY FEATURES` and enable `Recall Mode (Legacy)`.

    ![Enabling Recall Mode (Legacy) in Legacy Features](image/1bab9ccf0ec00f13.webp)

- Tap the study set's `More` menu in the top-right corner and turn on `Recall Mode (Legacy)`.

    ![Turning on Recall Mode (Legacy) from the study set menu](image/3c6a639b3199020d.webp)

- Once `Recall Mode (Legacy)` is on, occluded content is hidden.
- Try to recall the hidden content, then tap the occluded area to reveal it.
- Tap the area again to restore the occlusion.

![Animation: occluded content with legacy recall mode enabled](image/cb55d52b8c1a27b6.webp)

![Animation: tapping an occluded area to reveal its content](image/d95a43ef46645b32.webp)

## 3 Choose What to Hide for Recall Practice

### 3.1 Hide an Entire Excerpt

#### 3.1.1 Use `Excerpt Occlusion` to Hide the Whole Excerpt

- With `Excerpt Occlusion` enabled, select the `Text Excerpt Tool`, `Rectangle Excerpt Tool`, or `Lasso Excerpt Tool` and make an excerpt.

![The three excerpt tools: text, rectangle, and lasso](image/64d85a161b9e7b27.webp)

![Animation: hiding entire excerpts with the three excerpt tools](image/d9654b605ed6ebbe.webp)

#### 3.1.2 Turn Excerpt Occlusion On or Off

- Tap an **existing excerpt area**, then choose `Basic - More` in the pop-up menu to turn `Excerpt Occlusion` on or off.

![The Excerpt Occlusion toggle under Basic - More](image/a42a8445037ee45c.webp)

![Turning off excerpt occlusion](image/a22c06126da54407.webp)

### 3.2 Occlusion with `Emphasize`

- Select the `Rectangle Excerpt Tool` or `Text Excerpt Tool`, enable `Emphasize`, and excerpt the area you want. After excerpting, tap the characters you want to hide.

![Animation: using Emphasize with a rectangular excerpt](image/29520f982a7defc2.webp)

> **The Difference Between Emphasize Occlusion and Excerpt Occlusion**
>
> **1. Emphasize Occlusion**
>
> - Start with an excerpt and mark key areas within it for occlusion. **The key content remains within its full context**, making it easier to follow the surrounding logic.
> - When you add the card to review, the context is automatically retained, and the emphasized areas become clozes on the review card.
> - Best for content that **depends on context**, such as memorizing course material, explaining concepts, and studying ideas with closely connected reasoning.
>
> **2. Excerpt Occlusion**
>
> - Hides the entire excerpt, which can be a short passage, a single word, a formula, or an image. The resulting card **contains only the excerpted content**, without the surrounding sentences from the original document.
> - **Best for** content that **does not depend on context**, such as definitions, terminology, standalone formulas, and diagrams.

### 3.3 Three Ways to Create Occlusions and Clozes

#### 3.3.1 Handwriting Highlighter

Select the hand tool in the document, switch to the `highlighter`, and draw over the text or image area you want to hide.

![Animation: creating occlusions with the highlighter](image/881084bcc041f913.webp)

#### 3.3.2 Rectangle and Lasso Excerpts

- Select the `Rectangle Excerpt Tool` or `Lasso Excerpt Tool` and enable `Excerpt Occlusion` to hide areas containing **text, images, or handwritten annotations**.

![Enabling excerpt occlusion](image/065b5e3b148a4b07.webp)

![Animation: hiding handwritten annotations with a rectangular excerpt](image/9ee0e3d9b4c93e9a.webp)

#### 3.3.3 Text Excerpts

Select the `Text Excerpt Tool`, enable `Excerpt Occlusion`, and excerpt the text you want to hide.

![Enabling occlusion for text excerpts](image/19edce01c733e24c.webp)

![Animation: hiding a text excerpt](image/050123508c3c72e4.webp)
